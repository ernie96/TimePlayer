TimePlayer

A YouTube player and library for the keyboard. Search, play, subscribe and keep playlists, with NVDA reading it as it reads any other app and the playback position on your braille display.

Download TimePlayer from its latest release on GitHub

Opens the latest release on GitHub. No Google account is needed. Not made by or affiliated with YouTube.

What it does

Finding videos

Search for videos, playlists, Shorts or live streams, or paste any YouTube link. Featured videos and music charts for your region, and For You picks from what you watch, worked out on your computer.

Your library

Subscriptions with a What's New list of their latest uploads, Watch Later, likes, your own playlists, saved YouTube playlists, and a history that continues videos where you left off.

The player

Keys for seeking, chapters, speed and volume. Up next, comments, description, chapters, transcript and lyrics are each one key away. Captions and timed lyrics can be read aloud as they come.

Music

Control+M switches to Music: no video, a sound preset of its own, and music that keeps playing while you browse. Start a radio of songs like the one playing.

Skipping and sound

SponsorBlock skips or announces sponsor reads, and Return YouTube Dislike estimates dislikes. Sound presets and a custom equalizer, audio description when a video has it, and a choice of sound output.

Bringing your data

Import your subscriptions, history, likes, Watch Later and playlists by signing in to YouTube once, or from Google Takeout, NewPipe, LibreTube, FreeTube or OPML files. Download audio or video, one video or a whole list.

How it works with your screen reader

TimePlayer uses Windows' own controls, so your screen reader reads its lists and settings as it reads any other app. It stays quiet while you work and says what happened when you act, such as "Playing" or "Added to queue".

  • F1 says where you are and the keys that matter there. Ctrl+F1 gives the full help for that place.
  • With NVDA, a small add-on shows the playback position on a braille display, marks where chapters start, and lets a routing key jump anywhere in the video.
  • English, Romanian, Ukrainian and Spanish.

Which file to download

The latest release on GitHub has these files. The installer and the portable zip each include everything TimePlayer needs, and TimePlayer offers its own updates. It runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11; Mac and Linux versions are being worked on.

Installer

TimePlayer_setup.exe

Installs for your Windows user, without asking for administrator rights. Settings and library are kept in your user folder.

Portable

TimePlayer.zip

Extract the folder anywhere you can write to and run TimePlayer.exe. Settings and library stay in that folder.

Browser extension

TimePlayer-browser-extension.zip

Play in TimePlayer, for Chrome, Edge and Brave, sends YouTube links to TimePlayer. Settings, Links and browser, says how to add it.
